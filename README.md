## Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Performance Power BI Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed in Microsoft Power BI to analyze retail sales performance across multiple business dimensions.

The dashboard transforms retail transaction data into meaningful visual insights, enabling users to evaluate revenue performance across regions, stores, products, product categories, sales representatives, customer demographics, and time periods.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **data analysis, data modeling, DAX, KPI development, interactive reporting, and business-focused data visualization**.

---

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide a centralized view of retail sales performance and identify the key factors contributing to revenue generation.

The dashboard is designed to help answer questions such as:

- What is the overall revenue performance?
- Which regions generate the highest revenue?
- Which stores contribute most to sales?
- Which products and categories perform best?
- Which sales representatives generate the highest revenue?
- How does revenue vary across customer demographics?
- What are the monthly sales trends?
- Which days of the week generate the highest revenue?
- How can sales performance be monitored through interactive KPIs?

## Data Analysis Process

The project followed a structured business intelligence workflow:

1. Imported the retail sales dataset into Power BI.
2. Reviewed and prepared the source data.
3. Created analytical measures using DAX.
4. Developed KPI calculations for high-level performance monitoring.
5. Organized the report into business-focused visual sections.
6. Applied interactive filtering through slicers.
7. Created comparative and trend-based visualizations.
8. Designed the final dashboard for clear and efficient business reporting.
9. Interpreted the visual outputs to identify sales performance patterns.

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard provides a framework for identifying:

### Regional Performance

Revenue can be compared across regions to identify stronger and weaker geographic markets.

### Store Performance

Store-level analysis helps identify locations contributing significantly to overall revenue.

### Product Performance

Product-level analysis highlights high-performing and lower-performing products, supporting inventory and sales strategy decisions.

### Category Performance

Comparing product categories provides insight into which categories contribute most significantly to revenue.

### Sales Representative Performance

Sales representative analysis helps evaluate individual contribution to overall sales performance.

### Customer Demographics

Revenue can be segmented by customer gender to identify differences in purchasing contribution.

### Time-Based Performance

Monthly and weekday analysis provides visibility into sales patterns and periods of stronger or weaker revenue performance.
